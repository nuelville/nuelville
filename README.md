## 🔐 Frameworks & Standards

I am developing my understanding of widely used cybersecurity frameworks and compliance standards, with a focus on how their requirements relate to enterprise IT infrastructure and security operations.

| Framework / Standard | Area of Interest |
|---|---|
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) | Cybersecurity risk governance, identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery |
| NIST SP 800-53 | Security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information Security Management Systems (ISMS) and risk-based security management |
| SOC 2 | Assessment of service organization controls relevant to security and other applicable trust services criteria |
| HIPAA | Safeguarding protected health information in applicable healthcare environments |
| PCI DSS | Security requirements for environments that store, process, or transmit payment card data |

**My approach:** I aim to understand not only what each framework requires, but also how its controls can be translated into practical technical safeguards, documented procedures, measurable evidence, and sustainable business processes.
